The Brazilian Blow Dry

Our most requested treatment. A formaldehyde-free keratin smoothing treatment that leaves hair frizz-free, glossy and dramatically faster to style — and it lasts.

Every blow dry is tailored to your hair length, and we use the Vish system to measure exactly how much professional product your hair needs. A £20 surcharge applies because the formaldehyde-free formula needs longer in the chair.
